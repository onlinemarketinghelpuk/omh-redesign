04 / Process

How it goes

Most of our clients are first-timers. Here's every stage, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Start with a site visit
  1. Day 1

    Free site visit

    Ben or Sam comes round, measures up, looks at the drains and the neighbours' walls, and gives you a ballpark before leaving.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4

    Drawings, priced as they're drawn

    Our partner architects draw it. We price each version so the design stays inside your budget.

  3. Weeks 4 to 12

    Planning and party wall

    We submit planning or a prior approval, serve party wall notices and book Building Control.

  4. Contract

    Fixed price, fixed date

    One price, one completion date, and the £150 a day clause in writing. 5% deposit, no more than £5,000.

  5. On site

    Build

    One site manager for the whole job. A weekly plan on a whiteboard in your hallway, and a WhatsApp group with photos every day.

  6. Handover

    Keys, certificates, guarantee

    Building Control completion certificate, electrical certs, your 10-year guarantee, and a return visit at six months.

05 / Guarantee

Overruns are the builder's problem. Not yours.

Budgets blow up and dates slip because nobody's on the hook. We put ourselves on it, in the contract.

  • Price fixed at signing. It only changes if you change the design.
  • £150 for every working day past the completion date, paid before your final invoice.
  • Groundworks risk shown as a separate sum. Anything unused comes back to you.
  • Stage payments only for work that's finished. Never ahead.
  • 10-year insurance-backed guarantee through the FMB.

07 / FAQ

Questions we get every week

In 2026, expect £2,600 to £3,800 per m² for a single-storey extension including VAT, before kitchen units. A typical 25 m² kitchen extension in Kingston or Surbiton lands between £70,000 and £95,000. Two-storey works out cheaper per m² because the foundations and roof are shared across two floors.

Not always. Many single-storey rear extensions are permitted development: up to 3 m deep on a semi or terrace, or up to 6 m through the prior approval process. Side returns, wraparounds and two-storey extensions usually need full planning. Flats and homes in some conservation areas have no permitted development rights. Kingston and Richmond take around eight weeks to decide.

If you're digging foundations near a neighbour's or building on the boundary, the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 means notices go out before work starts. We serve them for you. If a neighbour appoints their own surveyor, budget £1,000 to £2,500 per neighbour, which you pay by law.

A 5% deposit capped at £5,000, then stage payments at fixed milestones: foundations, watertight shell, first fix, plastered, and completion. You only pay for a stage once it's finished and you've seen it. Any late-day payment comes off the last invoice.

Almost everyone does. We build the new shell first and only knock through once it's watertight, usually around week eight. We'll set up a temporary kitchen, and the knock-through week is the dusty one. We sheet everything and clean every evening.

Tree roots and old drains are common round here. Your contract shows a separate groundworks sum, usually £3,000 to £6,000, based on a trial hole we dig before you sign. If we don't use it, it's deducted from your final invoice.
