06 FAQ Q.01 to Q.06

Straight answers

If your question is about a specific building, send us the address and floor plan and we'll come back within a day.

In Manchester in 2026, Cat A typically runs £45 to £75 per sq ft and Cat B £70 to £150, excluding VAT. The biggest swing factors are how much M&E needs changing, the number of enclosed rooms, and whether furniture and AV are in our scope.

For 10,000 sq ft of Cat B, allow 3 to 5 weeks for design and procurement and 8 to 12 weeks on site. Long-lead items like glazed partitions and air handling units drive the programme, so we order them the week the contract is signed.

Yes. We phase the floor, work evenings and weekends where needed, and keep noisy work out of office hours. About a third of our projects are fully live. Out-of-hours labour costs more, and we'll show you that line clearly in the price.

Internal office works rarely need planning permission, but they do need Building Regulations approval, which we handle through an approved inspector. You'll almost certainly need a Licence for Alterations from your landlord. We prepare the drawings and method statements for it. A change of use, for example office to café, does need planning.

No upfront deposit. We submit monthly valuations for work completed, with 30-day terms and 3% retention: half released at practical completion and half after the 12-month defects period. Long-lead items over £25,000 may need paying on order, covered by a vesting certificate so they're legally yours.

Both. Our two in-house designers can take you from space plan to finishes, or we'll build to your architect's drawings under a traditional contract. The design fee is credited against the build if you go ahead with us.
