03 · How a mug is made

Three weeks, two firings and one potter's thumb.

This is why we make in batches, and why we tell you the posting date before you pay.

  1. Day 1

    Throw

    Cornish stoneware clay, thrown on the wheel to a weighed 520g.

  2. Day 2

    Trim and handle

    Turned when leather hard. Handles pulled by hand, never cast.

  3. Days 3 to 10

    Dry slowly

    A week under cloth, so nothing cracks.

  4. Day 11

    Bisque firing

    First firing to 1,000°C turns clay into ceramic.

  5. Day 13

    Glaze

    Dipped in Tide glaze, mixed in the studio from our own recipe.

  6. Day 15 to 18

    Glaze firing

    To 1,280°C, then two days cooling before we open the kiln.
