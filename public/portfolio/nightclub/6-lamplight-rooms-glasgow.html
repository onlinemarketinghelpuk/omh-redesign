What’s on

Three nights a week, none of them rushed.

    Door policy and house rules

    A wee word at the door.

    Nothing here is unusual, but it saves a disappointed walk back up Wilson Street. If you are unsure about anything, phone us on 0141 496 0127 before you set off.

    In your pocket

    • Real photo ID. The card or passport itself, in date.
    • Your e-ticket. On your phone is fine, brightness up.
    • A card or phone to pay with. The bars and cloakroom are cashless.
    Over 18s only
    Every night, in every room, private hires included. There are no under-18 events at Lamplight Rooms.
    Challenge 25
    If you look under 25 you will be asked for ID. It is a mandatory condition of every premises licence in Scotland under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, so please don’t take it personally.
    ID we accept
    A passport, a photocard driving licence, or a card carrying the PASS hologram (a Young Scot card, for example). It has to be the original and in date. No photos, screenshots or photocopies of ID.
    The door team
    Our door staff are SIA-licensed and wear their licences where you can see them. They are there to keep the night kind as well as safe.
    Searches
    Agreeing to a search of pockets and bags is a condition of entry. You can ask for it to be done by someone of the same gender, and away from the queue.
    Right of admission
    We reserve it. If you have had too much before you arrive, the answer will be a polite no: we are not allowed to admit or serve anyone who is drunk.
    Last entry 01:30
    Ticket or not, nobody comes in after 01:30. Music stops at 03:00 and the building is clear shortly after.
    Re-entry
    Before 01:30, get a stamp from the door team and you can come back. After 01:30 there is no re-entry. The smoking courtyard is inside the venue, so you do not need to leave for it.
    What to wear
    Whatever you can dance in. There is no dress code, with two exceptions that Glasgow will recognise: no football colours, and nothing with a slogan that picks a fight.
    Zero tolerance
    Drugs, harassment and discrimination of any kind get you removed and barred, and we involve the police where we need to. If someone is bothering you, tell any member of staff.

    Safety and welfare

    Looked after, start to finish.

    A good night is one everybody gets home from happy. Two trained welfare staff work every shift alongside the door team and the bar, and any of them will stop what they are doing to help.

    • Ask for Angela

      If you feel unsafe, or a date is not going the way you hoped, ask any member of staff for “Angela”. They will take you somewhere quiet and help you get home or get help, with no fuss made at the bar.

    • The welfare team and the Quiet Room

      Look for the lanyards marked Welfare. The Quiet Room is off the cloakroom corridor: low light, soft seats, water, phone chargers, and someone to sit with you for as long as you need.

    • If you think a drink has been spiked

      Tell the nearest member of staff straight away, for yourself or for a friend. We will stay with you and keep the drink. In an emergency we call 999. You can report it to the police afterwards on 101, and we will back you up.

    • Free tap water, always

      At every bar, all night, with no need to buy anything. Help yourself from the jugs or ask whoever is serving.

    • Earplugs, haze and strobes

      Free earplugs at every bar and the cloakroom. We use theatrical haze in all rooms and occasional strobe lighting in the Parlour. The Scullery and the Coal Cellar are strobe-free.

    • Home in one piece

      On your own at the end of the night? Tell the welfare team. We will wait with you inside until your booked taxi is at the door, and we can call one for you.

    Arrange access
    Step-free route
    The Wilson Street door has fourteen steps. The step-free entrance is on Brunswick Lane, with a platform lift down to the basement. All three rooms are level from there.
    Accessible toilets
    Two, beside the cloakroom, kept unlocked. There is a gender-neutral toilet block next to the Scullery.
    Free personal-assistant ticket
    If you need someone with you, their ticket is free. We don’t ask for paperwork at the door.
    Seats, queues and quiet
    We can hold a seat near the dancefloor, bring you in ahead of the queue, and the Quiet Room is yours whenever you want a break.

    Taxis

    Use a licensed taxi from a rank or one you have hailed, or a private hire car you have booked yourself. A private hire car that has not been booked is not insured to carry you, so never get into one that simply pulls up. The door team can phone a taxi for you and you are welcome to wait inside.

    Driving

    Please don’t. There is no parking at the venue and the Merchant City is busy at night. Blue Badge holders can be dropped at the Brunswick Lane entrance.

    Please leave quietly. Our neighbours are asleep.

    Folk live above us and across the lane. Keep voices down on Wilson Street, don’t hang about under windows, and take the party home with you.
