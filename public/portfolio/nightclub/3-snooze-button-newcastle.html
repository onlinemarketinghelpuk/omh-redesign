        Door policy & house rules

        Easy to get in, if you bring the right card.

        Our door team want you inside as much as you do. Read this once and the queue is the hardest part of your night.

        Challenge 25

        Look under 25? You'll be asked for one of these. The real thing, in your hand.

        • Passport
        • Photocard driving licence
        • PASS-hologram proof-of-age card
        • No photos, screenshots or photocopies of ID

        1. Over 18s only

          No exceptions, student nights included. Challenge 25 applies at the door and at every bar.

        2. Student ID is a second card

          A valid student ID gets you the student price. It isn't proof of age unless it carries the PASS hologram, so bring both.

        3. A licensed door team

          Every member of the team is SIA licensed. Searches of bags, pockets and coats are a condition of entry, and we reserve the right of admission.

        4. Last entry is 01:30

          Doors 22:30, last entry 01:30, home time 03:00. Early-bird and standard tickets have their own arrival times printed on them.

        5. No re-entry after 00:30

          Before then, get a hand stamp on your way out. The smoking terrace is inside the venue, so you won't need to leave for it.

        6. No dress code

          Shoes and a top, and you're dressed. Fancy dress and team kit are welcome on Wednesdays. Full-face masks come off at the door.

        7. Zero tolerance

          Drugs, harassment and discrimination of any kind get you removed and barred. Anything illegal is passed to the police.

        8. If you've had enough

          We can't admit or serve anyone who is clearly drunk. That's the law. We'll get you water and help you get home instead.

        Safety & welfare

        Everyone gets home. That's the whole plan.

        A good night out is one you remember for the right reasons. Here's what we do, and what to do if something feels wrong.

        "Is Angela working tonight?"

        Ask for Angela

        If you feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or just need to get away from someone, ask any member of staff for Angela. They'll take you somewhere quiet, away from the dance floor, and help you get home.

        A welfare team, every night

        Two trained welfare staff in lime vests from doors to close. You'll find them by the main staircase, or any bartender can radio for them.

        The Nap Room

        Our safe space: a quiet, staffed room next to the cloakroom with seating, water, phone chargers and someone to sit with you until you're ready.

        Think a drink's been spiked?

        Tell the nearest member of staff straight away. We'll stay with you, keep the drink and get medical help. Drink covers are free at every bar.

        999in an emergency 101to report it afterwards

        Free tap water

        At every bar, all night, no purchase needed. Just ask. There's a self-serve water point on each landing too.

        Free earplugs

        From the cloakroom and every bar. Strobe lighting, lasers, haze and CO₂ jets are used on all three floors.

        Getting here & home

        Seven minutes from everything.

        We're in the middle of town, between the station and St James. Getting here is easy. Plan the way home before you come out.

        Metro and train

        Central Station (Metro and National Rail) is a 6-minute walk, St James 5 and Monument 7. Last Metro trains leave around midnight, so check times before you travel.

        Night buses

        Late and night services stop on Grainger Street and at the Monument, both under 7 minutes away. Haymarket bus station is a 12-minute walk.

        Taxis

        Use a licensed taxi or private hire you have booked. The marshalled rank on the Bigg Market is 3 minutes away. Never get into a car you didn't book.

        Please leave quietly

        Our neighbours are asleep. Keep the singing for the Mixtape and the chat for the walk home.

        The practical bits

        Cloakroom

        £2 per item for coats and bags, card only. It's on the ground floor, to the left of the stairs. Keep your ticket: no ticket, no coat until close.

        Lost property

        Found items are kept for 28 days. Email lost@snoozebutton.example with what, when and which floor.

        Cashless bars

        Card and phone payments only at every bar, the cloakroom and the door. There's no cash machine in the building.

        Photography

        Our photographer is in most nights and photos go on our channels. Don't want yours used? Tell them on the night or email hello@snoozebutton.example.
