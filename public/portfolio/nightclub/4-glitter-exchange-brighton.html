Door policy & house rules

Everyone’s welcome. Bigotry isn’t.

We are an LGBTQ+ venue, run by and for our community, and friends are welcome as guests in our house. The rules are short, and they are the same for everybody.

Zero tolerance of homophobia, transphobia, biphobia, racism, ableism and harassment of any kind.

If anyone makes you uncomfortable, tell any member of staff. We will believe you, and we will act. People who break this rule are asked to leave and may be barred.
How we look after you

  1. Over 18s only

    Every night, on every floor, with no exceptions.

  2. Challenge 25

    If you look under 25, expect to be asked for ID. We accept a passport, a photocard driving licence or a card with the PASS hologram. Photos and screenshots of ID are not accepted. Your ID doesn’t need to match the name you use or how you present: we check your age and nothing else.

  3. Our door team

    Our door supervisors are SIA-licensed and wear their licences where you can see them. Bag and pocket searches are a condition of entry. You can ask to be searched by a team member of the gender you are most comfortable with.

  4. Right of admission

    Management reserves the right to refuse entry. If you’ve had too much before you arrive, we will ask you to come back another night. It is never about what you are wearing or who you are with.

  5. Last entry and re-entry

    Last entry is 01:30 Thursday to Saturday and midnight on Sunday. There is no re-entry after 00:30. The smoking terrace is inside the venue, so you won’t need to leave.

  6. Dress code

    There isn’t one. Sequins, trainers, a ballgown, your work fleece: all correct. We only ask for shoes on the dance floor and nothing that carries a hateful message.

  7. Zero tolerance of drugs

    Anyone found with illegal drugs will be refused entry or asked to leave, and we may call the police. If you or a friend feel unwell, tell us straight away: asking for help will never get you into trouble.

  8. Ask first

    Ask before you touch, ask before you photograph, and take no for an answer the first time. Performers are at work: applaud loudly, tip generously and keep your hands to yourself.

Safety & welfare

Looked after, from first drink to last dance.

There is a welfare host on every shift, in a gold sash so you can find them across a dark room. They aren’t security. They are there to help.

  • Ask for Angela

    If you feel unsafe, on a date that is going wrong, or being bothered, ask any member of staff for Angela. They will take you somewhere private and help you get home or back to your friends.

  • A welfare host on every shift

    Trained in first aid, vulnerability, and drug and alcohol awareness. Look for the gold sash, or ask at either bar and they will come to you.

  • The Quiet Room

    Low light, soft seats and no music, just off the foyer. Stay for five minutes or an hour. Water, phone chargers and earplugs are on the side.

  • If you think a drink was spiked

    Tell staff straight away and stay with someone you trust. We will look after you and keep the drink. In an emergency call 999; to report it afterwards, call 101.

  • Free tap water at every bar

    In a proper glass, without buying anything first. After midnight there are self-serve jugs at the end of both bars.

  • Earplugs, strobes and haze

    Free earplugs at both bars and the cloakroom. The disco uses strobe lighting and haze; the Cabaret Bar and the Quiet Room never do.

Emergency: call 999. To report something after the night, call 101, or write to us at welfare@glitterexchange.example.

Getting here & home

Five minutes from the sea, two from the bus.

The Glitter Exchange18 Madeira Mews, Kemptown, Brighton BN2 1GX

Sketch map, not to scale. Main doors are on Madeira Mews; the step-free entrance is ten metres to the left.

By train

Brighton station is about 20 minutes on foot, mostly downhill, or 8 minutes by bus. Last trains towards London leave around midnight on most nights, so check before you travel.

By bus and night bus

Buses stop on St James’s Street, two minutes away, and at Old Steine, five minutes away. Night buses leave from Old Steine on Fridays and Saturdays; check the operator’s timetable for tonight.

By taxi

Use a licensed taxi, or private hire you have booked. Never get into a car that simply offers you a lift. The nearest rank is by Old Steine, and our door team will happily wait with you until yours arrives.

On foot, by bike or by car

Ten minutes from the pier along Marine Parade. Cycle stands are right outside on Madeira Mews. There is no parking at the venue; Blue Badge holders can be dropped at the step-free entrance.

Please leave quietly. Our neighbours are asleep, and we would like to keep them as neighbours.

Good to know

Coats, cards, cameras and lost sequins.

  • Cloakroom

    £2.50 an item, by the box office, open until close. Nothing bigger than a weekend bag, please: we have sequins to store.

  • Lost property

    We keep finds for 28 days. Email lost@glitterexchange.example with a description and the night you were in.

  • Cashless bars

    Both bars, the cloakroom and the box office take card and contactless only. There is no cash machine in the building.

  • Photography

    Our photographer wears a gold lanyard and always asks first. No photos in the toilets or the Quiet Room. Want a picture taken down? Email us and it goes.

The small print

Privacy

We collect only what we need to sell you a ticket, answer an enquiry or send the newsletter you asked for. We never sell or swap your details. Booking records are kept for 24 months, enquiry emails for 12. You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, at privacy@glitterexchange.example, and you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies it needs to work, such as remembering what is in your booking. We do not use advertising cookies. If we ever add analytics, we will ask you first and “no” will be as easy to choose as “yes”.

Terms

Tickets are sold to over-18s and are non-refundable unless we cancel or move a night. Every price shown includes the booking fee. Entry is always subject to our door policy and house rules. Full terms are sent with each booking confirmation.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It works with a keyboard and with screen readers, at 200% zoom, and it respects your reduced-motion setting; moving elements can be paused. If something does not work for you, tell us at access@glitterexchange.example and we will put it right.
