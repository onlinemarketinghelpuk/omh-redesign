The venue

Three rooms under a Mayfair townhouse, 22 steps down.

Sablier holds 320 people across a main room, a vaulted bar and a private cellar. It was built for sitting down as much as for dancing, so every room has somewhere to land.

Door policy & house rules

A calm door makes a better night.

Entry is by guest list or table booking. If there is room we welcome walk-ins too, at the door team’s discretion. These are the rules they work to, so nobody is surprised on the pavement.

“Good evening. Guest list or table?”What you will be asked at the door

  1. Over 18s only

    Sablier is strictly over 18, every night, including table guests and private parties.

  2. Challenge 25

    If you look under 25 you will be asked for ID. We accept a passport, a photocard driving licence or a card with the PASS hologram. Photos, screenshots and photocopies of ID are not accepted.

  3. The door team

    Our door team are SIA licensed. Searches are a condition of entry, and the management reserves the right of admission.

  4. Dress code: smart

    Dress for a night in Mayfair: tailoring, dresses, shirts, smart shoes or clean leather trainers. No sportswear and no caps. If you are unsure, ask us before you travel.

  5. Last entry and re-entry

    Last entry is 01:00 for everyone, guest list and tables included. There is no re-entry after 01:00. Before then, the smoking terrace on Quill Yard is inside our cordon, so you never leave the venue to use it.

  6. Zero tolerance

    No drugs, no harassment and no discrimination of any kind. Anyone involved is removed and refused future entry, and we involve the police where the law requires it.

  7. Service is our call

    We do not serve anyone who appears drunk, and we may suggest a glass of water and a seat. It is the law, and it is also good manners.

Safety & welfare

Looked after, from the stairs to the taxi.

Every member of staff is trained to help, and two of them do nothing else all night.

Ask for Angela

If you feel unsafe, uncomfortable or threatened, ask any member of staff for Angela. They will take you somewhere private and help you get home or get help, without a fuss.

Welfare team and the quiet room

Two trained welfare staff are on the floor every night, in champagne-coloured armbands. The quiet room beside the cloakroom has seating, water and phone chargers.

If you think a drink was spiked

Tell a member of staff straight away and stay with someone you trust. We keep the drink, get medical help and support you in reporting it. In an emergency call 999. To report it afterwards, call 101.

Free tap water

Free tap water at every bar, at any time of night. You never need to buy a drink to ask for it.

Earplugs, strobe and haze

Free earplugs at the cloakroom and both bars. We use haze, moving lights and occasional strobe lighting, mainly after midnight in the Salon.

Tell us afterwards

Something not right about your night? Email welfare@sablier.example. Every report is logged, read by the general manager and answered.

  • By Tube

    Green Park is six minutes on foot and Bond Street is eight. On Friday and Saturday nights the Night Tube runs on the Central, Jubilee, Piccadilly and Victoria lines, so both stations are open when we close.

  • Night buses

    The N9, N19, N22, N38 and N97 stop on Piccadilly by Green Park station. Check times with Transport for London before you set off.

  • Taxis

    Use a licensed taxi or a private hire car you have booked. Wait inside by the cloakroom and the door team will tell you when it arrives. Never get into a car that approaches you on the street.

  • Leaving

    Please leave quietly, our neighbours are asleep. Quill Yard is a residential mews and sound carries further than you would think.

Practical things

  • Cloakroom

    £3 per item, at the foot of the stairs. Coats, bags and umbrellas are fine; suitcases cannot be stored. Keep your numbered tag.

  • Lost property

    Kept for 28 days. Email lost@sablier.example with the date and a description. Collection is Wednesday to Saturday from 20:00.

  • Cashless bars

    All three bars and the cloakroom take card and contactless only. We do not hold cash on the premises.

  • Photography

    We photograph some nights for our own channels. Tell the photographer or any member of staff if you would rather not be in shot. No flash on the floor, please.
