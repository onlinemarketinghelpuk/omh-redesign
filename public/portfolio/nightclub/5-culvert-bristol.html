    The rig & the arches

    Eighteen eighteens.

    Culvert was built around its sound system, not the other way round. Every cabinet was cut, glued and tuned by hand in a lock-up off Feeder Road, then voiced in the arches over two winters. No off-the-shelf boxes, no badges on the grilles. Just birch ply, eighteen 18-inch subs and a lot of patience.

    Protect your ears

    Ringing is not a souvenir.

    We built this system to be felt in your chest. That is exactly why we want you in earplugs. A good pair takes the edge off the top end and leaves the bass where it belongs. If your ears ring on the way home, that was damage, and it adds up.

    Door policy & house rules

    Read this before you queue.

    Eight rules, written plainly. They are the same for everyone, including our mates, the DJs' mates and you.

    • Passport
    • Photocard driving licence
    • PASS-hologram card
    Still unsure? Read the FAQ

    1. Over 18s only

      No exceptions, and that includes under-18s who arrive with an adult. If we cannot confirm your age you will not be coming in, and the ticket will not be refunded.

    2. Challenge 25

      If you look under 25, expect to be asked for ID. We accept a passport, a photocard driving licence or a card carrying the PASS hologram. Originals only: no photos, photocopies or screenshots of ID.

    3. Searches on entry

      Our door team are SIA-licensed. Everyone is searched as a condition of entry, bags included, and we use metal-detecting wands. You can always ask to be searched by someone of the same gender.

    4. Right of admission

      The management reserves the right of admission and the door supervisor's decision is final. If you are too drunk to look after yourself we will turn you away, find you some water and help you get home.

    5. Last entry 02:00

      Doors 22:00, last entry 02:00, music off at 05:00. On Sunday Weight it is doors 16:00, last entry 21:00, finish 23:00. A ticket does not override last entry.

    6. No re-entry

      Once you are in, you are in. The smoking yard is inside the venue, so you will not need to leave. If you have a medical or access reason to step out, speak to the door supervisor first.

    7. No dress code

      Wear what you can dance in. The floor is concrete, so closed shoes are sensible. No bags bigger than A4, no glass, no outside drinks, and no professional cameras without accreditation.

    8. Zero tolerance

      Drugs: anyone found with them is refused entry or removed, and dealing is reported to the police. Harassment and discrimination of any kind, including racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism and unwanted touching: you are out, and you are not coming back.

    Safety & welfare

    Look after each other.

    A good night is one everybody gets home from. Two trained welfare staff in orange lanyards work every session, and every member of the team, from the bar to the booth, knows what to do if you need help.

    Something wrong?

    Tell any member of staff, at any time. In an emergency call 999. To report something afterwards, call 101.

    Ask for Angela

    If you feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or a date is not going the way you hoped, go to any bar or any member of staff and ask for Angela. They will take you somewhere quiet and help you get sorted. No explanation needed.

    Welfare team & the Dry Room

    The Dry Room, beside the cloakroom, is our safe space: low light, seats, water, phone chargers and someone to talk to. Come for five minutes or the rest of the night. Nobody will ask why.

    Drink spiking

    If you think you or a friend has been spiked, tell the nearest member of staff straight away and do not leave alone. We will stay with you, keep the drink and get help. Call 999 in an emergency, and report it on 101.

    Free tap water

    Free tap water at every bar, always, with no need to buy anything. There is also a self-serve tap beside the Arch Two bar, so you never have to queue for it.

    Earplugs, strobes & haze

    Free earplugs at both bars, the cloakroom and the Dry Room. We use strobe lighting, lasers and theatrical haze at club nights. Arch Two never uses strobes, and Sunday Weight is strobe-free throughout.

    Getting here & home

    Six minutes from platform 3.

    We are under the main line just east of Temple Meads. Look for the two arches with the orange lamp over the door, and listen.

    • By train

      Bristol Temple Meads is a six-minute walk: main exit, down to Temple Gate, left along Cattle Market Road, left into Sidings Lane. Lawrence Hill station is 15 minutes on foot.

    • By bus

      Most city services stop at Temple Gate. Night buses leave from Temple Gate and Old Market through the small hours; check times with the operator before you travel.

    • By bike

      Forty covered stands in the yard, lit and on camera, inside the door team's line of sight. Bring your own lock.

    • By car or taxi

      There is no parking at the arches and Sidings Lane is closed to traffic on club nights. Drop-offs and pick-ups are from the marked bay on Cattle Market Road.

    Getting home

    Use a licensed taxi or a private hire car you have booked yourself. Never get into a car that approaches you. If your phone has died, the door team will call one for you and you can wait inside.

    Please leave quietly. Our neighbours are asleep. Keep the singing for the dance floor and take your rubbish with you.
