What we do

More than a food parcel.

Food gets people through the door. Advice, a warm room and a friendly face are what help them out of the hole.

  • Emergency food

    Three days of food for your household, chosen by you, including fresh veg and toiletries.

    Tue and Thu, 10am to 2pm

  • Community pantry

    Pay £4.50 a week and take around £20 of shopping. A membership, not a handout, for anyone on a low income.

    Tue, Thu and Sat

  • Saturday hot lunch

    Two courses at a shared table, cooked by volunteers from the Avenue's restaurants. Free, and kids eat first.

    Sat, 11am to 2pm

  • Money and benefits advice

    Free one-to-one sessions with a trained adviser. Last year they helped families claim £284,000 they were owed.

    By appointment

  • Baby bank

    Nappies, formula, clothes and prams for families with children under five, referred by midwives and health visitors.

    Thu, 10am to 2pm

  • Home deliveries

    Food delivered by volunteer drivers to people who are housebound, unwell or caring for someone.

    Wednesdays, across Hull

    Getting help

    What happens when you come in

    Most people tell us the hardest bit was walking through the door. Here's exactly what to expect, so there are no surprises.

    1. Walk in

      No appointment and no referral for your first three visits. Just say hello at the front desk.

    2. Have a cuppa

      A volunteer sits with you for a few minutes. We'll ask how many people you're feeding and about any allergies.

    3. Choose your food

      You pick from the shelves, like a shop. Fresh fruit, veg, bread and toiletries too.

    4. Get extra help

      If you'd like, book a free chat about money, benefits or energy bills. Totally optional.

    Prefer to keep it private? We have a side entrance on Ella Street and a quiet room. Just ring ahead and ask for Sam.
