How we choose

From 142 ideas to 11 funded projects.

Our last funding round, step by step. People living with MND sit on the panel that makes the final call.

142

Open call for ideas

Any UK researcher can apply. Applications open each March.

58

Scientific shortlist

Our research committee checks each idea is sound, original and about MND.

24

Independent peer review

At least three outside experts score each proposal, following AMRC best practice.

16

Lay panel

Eight people living with or caring for someone with MND ask: will this matter to patients?

11

Funded and followed

Trustees award the grants. Every team reports to us every six months.

Our promise

89p

Of every £1 goes into research. If that ever drops below 85p, we'll tell every donor, in writing, and explain why.

What you can expect from us

  • Only the best science

    Every pound goes through independent peer review. No pet projects, no favours.

  • A report back, once a year

    One email each spring showing what your money did. Not weekly appeals.

  • No cold calls, ever

    We never phone donors to ask for more, and we never sell or swap your details.

  • Change or stop any time

    Monthly gifts can be paused or cancelled online in under a minute.

If you pay UK Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax, Gift Aid lets us claim an extra 25p from HMRC for every £1 you give, at no cost to you. You need to have paid at least as much tax in the year as we claim.
