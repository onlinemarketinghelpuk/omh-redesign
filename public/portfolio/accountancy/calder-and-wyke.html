03 · How we workp. 03

From first phone call to your first monthly pack, in five chapters.

  1. Chapter one
    Day 1 · 30 minutes

    A proper conversation

    Helen or James calls you, not a salesperson. We ask what keeps you up, and what your current accountant doesn't tell you.

  2. Chapter two
    Week 1 to 2 · free

    Health check

    We read your last two years' accounts and tax returns and send you a short written note, whether or not you become a client.

  3. Chapter three
    Week 2

    Fixed-fee proposal

    One monthly fee covering everything agreed, written down. No hourly billing and no charge for phone calls.

  4. Chapter four
    Weeks 3 to 6

    The switch

    We request professional clearance from your old accountant and move your Xero or QuickBooks setup over. You sign one letter.

  5. Chapter five
    Every month after

    The rhythm

    Accounts by the 8th working day, a call to walk through them, and a quarterly review in person or on Teams.

04 · Our promisesp. 04

No surprises. In writing.

The worst thing an accountant can do is tell you about a tax bill a month before it's due. These are in every engagement letter we sign.

Late accounts are free

If your management accounts aren't with you by the 8th working day, that month's fee is waived. We've waived it twice since 2019.

Tax bills twelve months ahead

Every quarter you get an updated estimate of your company and personal tax, with the date each one is due.

One fee, reviewed once a year

Agreed in advance, paid monthly by direct debit. Ring us as often as you like. It won't appear on an invoice.

A partner on every account

You'll deal with Helen Calder or James Wyke directly, plus one named manager. Not a different junior each year.
