FAQ

Questions we're often asked.

Can't see your question here? Get in touch and you'll speak to a strategist, not a sales team.

0161 496 0732 hello@flocksocial.co.uk
Is there a minimum contract?

Yes, three months. That's how long it takes to test, learn and show meaningful results. After that, retainers roll monthly and you can leave with 30 days' notice.

Is ad spend included in the retainer?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms from your own ad accounts, so you always own the data and can see exactly where every pound goes.

Who owns the content you create?

You do. Once invoices are paid, the photography, video and copy we make for you is yours to use anywhere. Creator content comes with the usage rights agreed in each creator's contract.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within two to three weeks of your audit. New content is normally live by week three, and paid campaigns can start sooner if you already have assets ready.

Do you only work with Manchester brands?

No. We're based in Ancoats but work with brands across the UK. Most meetings happen on video calls, and we travel for shoots.

How do you handle influencer disclosure?

Every paid or gifted creator post is clearly labelled as an ad, in line with ASA and CMA guidance. We brief creators on the rules and check posts before they go live.

What's included in the free social audit?

A review of up to four of your channels, your content and three competitors, followed by a short written report and a 30-minute call. There's no charge and no obligation.

How do you report on results?

You'll get a monthly report and call covering reach, engagement, website visits, leads and sales, plus what we'll change next. Full-service clients also get a live dashboard.
