Process

Your first 90 days, logged.

  1. day_00

    Free account audit

    We go through your ad accounts, analytics and CRM for an hour and send a written audit with the five biggest leaks. Yours to keep even if you don't hire us.

  2. day_07

    Tracking fixed

    Every form, call and booked demo tracked properly, with lead quality pushed back from your CRM using offline_conversions.

  3. day_14

    Campaigns rebuilt

    New structure, new ads and landing pages live. Wasted searches blocked from day one.

  4. day_30

    First monthly review

    A 30-minute call with your account lead. One page of numbers: leads, cost per lead, pipeline. No 40-slide decks.

  5. day_90

    Scale or switch

    We move budget to what's producing revenue and cut what isn't. If cost per lead hasn't dropped, month four is on us.
